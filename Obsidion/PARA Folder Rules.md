# Directory Taxonomy & Classification Rules

Use the following folder taxonomy and classification logic to analyze, categorize, and organize files or notes.

## 1. Folder Definitions

* **00_Inbox**: Temporary staging area for unorganized or unprocessed files prior to categorization.
* **01_Projects**: Work or initiatives with a specific goal and a defined deadline or end date.
* **02_Areas**: Long-term domains or responsibilities requiring continuous maintenance without a fixed deadline.
* **03_Resources**: Topics of ongoing interest, references, or general knowledge that may be useful in the future.
* **04_Templates**: Reusable boilerplates, standard documents, or structural formats.
* **05_Archive**: Inactive, completed, or obsolete items preserved for historical reference from Projects, Areas, or Resources.
* **MOCs (Maps of Content)**: Indexing hubs or master navigation documents that link related notes and files across the workspace.

---

## 2. Classification Decision Logic

When processing a new file or task, apply the following decision flow in order:

1. **Unprocessed Item?** -> `00_Inbox`
2. **Reusable Format/Boilerplate?** -> `04_Templates`
3. **Index or Navigation Hub?** -> `MOCs`
4. **Completed or Inactive?** -> `05_Archive`
5. **Has a Specific Goal AND Deadline?** -> `01_Projects`
6. **Ongoing Responsibility (No Deadline)?** -> `02_Areas`
7. **Reference Material or General Topic?** -> `03_Resources`
